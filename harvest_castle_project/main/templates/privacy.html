Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Harvest Castle handles information you provide while using this website.

Last updated: 15 September 2026.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Harvest Castle handles information provided when you browse this website, place an order, or contact the business. It covers the current website and ordering flow only, and it will be updated if those practices change.

2. Information we may collect

Harvest Castle only asks for information needed to take and fulfil your order:

  • Name, phone number, and email address where you provide them.
  • Delivery information: hostel or building, faculty or office, room or block, off-campus address, landmark, and delivery instructions.
  • Order details: selected products, variants and weights, quantities, and order totals.
  • Messages you choose to send through WhatsApp or the Contact page.

3. How we use information

Information is used for practical, operational purposes:

  • Processing and confirming your orders.
  • Communicating with you about an order.
  • Coordinating on-campus or off-campus delivery.
  • Responding to questions sent through the website or WhatsApp.
  • Keeping order records needed to run the business.

4. WhatsApp communications

You may choose to contact Harvest Castle or place an order through WhatsApp. Anything you send that way travels through WhatsApp's platform, which Harvest Castle does not operate or control. Only share what you are comfortable sending by chat.

5. Payments

Online payment is not yet live on this website, and Harvest Castle does not currently collect or store payment-card details. If third-party payment processing is introduced in future, payment details will be handled by the selected payment provider, and this policy will be updated to describe that.

6. Delivery information

Delivery details are collected to identify where an order should go and to coordinate on-campus or off-campus delivery, including landmarks and instructions you provide. Harvest Castle does not collect GPS locations and does not track deliveries live.

7. Information sharing

Customer information is used by Harvest Castle and, where necessary to fulfil your request, by the service providers involved in that request — for example processing an order, coordinating a delivery, or completing a communication or payment service you chose. Information is not shared with advertisers, marketplaces, or unrelated third parties.

8. Data security

Reasonable measures are intended to protect the information you provide. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be promised.

9. Data retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate operational, legal, or business purposes, such as fulfilling orders and keeping order records.

10. Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and its scripts do not set tracking cookies. The site does use your browser's own storage for necessary functionality: your cart, your grid/list view preference, and your most recent order details are kept in your browser so the site works across pages. Clearing your browser storage removes them from your device.

11. Third-party services

Only services actually involved in your visit are relevant here:

  • WhatsApp, when you choose to contact Harvest Castle or order through it.
  • Font delivery used to display this website's text.

Future payment providers will be described here if and when they are integrated.

12. Children's privacy

Harvest Castle sells everyday farm produce to household customers and does not knowingly collect information directly from children. Orders should be placed by an adult.

13. Your rights and requests

To ask about information Harvest Castle holds about you, or to raise a privacy concern, contact Harvest Castle through the Contact page or on WhatsApp.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or business practices change. The Last Updated date above will change whenever a substantive update is made.
