About Harvest Castle

Harvest Castle connects you with fresh farm produce through a simple, direct ordering experience — online or on WhatsApp.

What Harvest Castle is

A farm-produce ordering business. Harvest Castle is a single-seller storefront: one business offering fresh vegetables, fruits and everyday staples directly to customers. There are no middlemen, no marketplace sellers and no complicated steps.

Two easy ways to order. Browse what is available on the website and check out there, or send your list directly through WhatsApp and order by chat — whichever suits you.

Made for the FUNAAB community. Harvest Castle is based just off campus and designed around students and staff around FUNAAB, Camp and Abeokuta. At checkout you share your hostel or address, and delivery or pickup is arranged personally with you.

Delivery & pickup

How you receive your order

Delivery is arranged personally with you — there is no automated courier system. When you order, share:

  • On campus: hostel or faculty/office, room or block, and any extra direction.
  • Off campus: full address, a nearby landmark, and delivery notes.
  • Or pickup: agree a pickup time at the store above.

Full delivery details will be collected at checkout or over WhatsApp.

Prefer to order by chat?

Send Harvest Castle your list on WhatsApp — produce, quantities and where you are — and we will confirm availability and delivery with you.
