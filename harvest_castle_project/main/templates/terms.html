5. Orders

You can order in two ways:

  • Website: browse, select a variant and quantity, add to cart, and submit your order at checkout.

A submitted order is a request, not a guarantee of every item: where necessary, Harvest Castle confirms availability with you before preparing anything.

Delivery is manually coordinated, not automated:
